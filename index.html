My Story

AI / Software Engineer

AI Engineer at SemiAI · UCSC Computer Science · Dec 2026

I started coding at Las Positas College after working as a freelance photographer. Photography taught me to frame a subject, establish visual hierarchy, and strip away noise until the important thing is unmistakable. I bring the same discipline to software: if a tool makes someone pause to figure out what matters, it still has work to do.

Today, I’m an AI Engineer at SemiAI, working on a nimble team building the company’s U.S. presence alongside our headquarters in Korea. I turn raw operational data into usable insight by building retrieval systems, shipping practical internal tools, and designing workflows that turn ambiguous inputs into clear next steps.

Outside work, I build lightweight native utilities, including macOS menu bar tools, and experiment with open models running locally on my own hardware. The fact that near-frontier reasoning can now run privately on a personal machine feels like a fundamental shift. It’s the boundary I want to keep building at. Away from screens, I play ultimate frisbee.

01Local retrieval · in progress

Airlock Harness

A question deserves a boundary.

The useful question is often a small one: What did this vendor quote? It should not require reading three proposals end to end—or trusting an answer that cannot show its work.

Airlock combines lexical and semantic retrieval, checks numeric and attribution support, then answers with evidence or refuses when the record is not enough.

The goal is not a system that always speaks. It is a system that earns the right to.
